Ronald Machaka

MIEAust — Professional Engineer & Innovation Scholar

Advanced Materials Engineer and independent AI researcher based in Newcastle, NSW. My work spans powder metallurgy, metal injection moulding, titanium alloy design, and machine learning applied to complex metallurgical systems — from predicting high-entropy alloy phase formation to decoding shape-memory thermal hysteresis through explainable ML.
